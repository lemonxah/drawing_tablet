# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** February 9, 2026

**Developer:** lemonxah

## Introduction

Drawing Tablet ("the App") is a utility that turns your Android tablet into a remote drawing tablet for Linux PCs. The App communicates with a companion server application running on your local network to stream your PC screen and relay stylus and touch input.

## Information Collection

The App does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

- No accounts or sign-ups are required
- No personal data (name, email, phone number, etc.) is collected
- No usage analytics or telemetry are collected
- No crash reports are sent to external services
- No advertising SDKs or ad networks are used

## Network Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

- **INTERNET** — Used to communicate with the Drawing Tablet server on your local network
- **ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE** — Used to check network connectivity status
- **ACCESS_WIFI_STATE** — Used for local network server discovery

All network communication occurs exclusively between the App and the companion server on your local network. **No data is transmitted to any external servers, third parties, or the internet.**

## Local Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences:

- Remembered server address (host and port)
- Auto-connect preference (on/off)

This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted externally. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the App's data through your device settings.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that collect user data.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since the App collects no personal information from any user, no special data handling is required.
